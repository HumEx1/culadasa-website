## 1. The situation

- culadasa.com is the legacy website of Culadasa (John Yates, author of *The Mind Illuminated*), who died in 2021. The site's WordPress title is "Inspiration Station".
- Elizabeth Pugh has kept it up since his death by paying the support company the site ended up with when it went down in his last days: **24x7 WP Support** (24x7wpsupport.com). It hosts the site and holds a support contract. There are "large payments needed for something every year", it has been expensive, and Elizabeth does not have time to maintain it.
- The site is paid up and will stay live for about another year from 20 November 2026. A plan is needed before then.
- No recent content edits. Elizabeth has admin access to the WordPress site and the hosting account and can make exports.
- Traffic (Elizabeth's screenshot, read by Jesse Fallon): roughly 60–100 visits a month, direct.

## 4. Ongoing maintenance group — D's proposal (22 November 2026)

Not yet built. No objections raised in the thread.

- **A new shared Google account**, e.g. [email removed], used as the contact and login email for everything below; password shared with the group. All passwords in a Google Sheet on that account, shared only with group members. Two-factor backup codes in a "Culadasa Website Info" Google Sheet. (D's judgement: stakes too low for a password manager.)
- **Domain:** every group member added as a user on the registrar account, auto-renew on (needs a credit card). If GoDaddy's access levels don't allow it, move the name to Namecheap under the new Gmail and share from there.
- **Website source:** a free GitHub organisation "Culadasa". GitHub does not allow shared logins, so each member creates a free GitHub account and is added as an owner.
- **YouTube:** every member given owner-level access to the channel (blocked until the Google account is recovered).
- **SoundCloud:** login moved to the new Gmail.
- **Patreon:** login moved to the new Gmail, or left with whoever runs it, so the information doesn't disappear with one person.
- **Backups:** site files on GitHub; anyone in the group can download a copy. D also asked Elizabeth to upload the whole SoundCloud backup drive to the Google Drive of the new account as a second, cloud copy.
- **Handover note:** a one-page guide to what's where and who has access, with no passwords in it.

D's open request to Elizabeth (23 Nov): GoDaddy login, SoundCloud login, and the latest WordPress export (now received).

## 7. Migration facts to keep in mind

- All video is on YouTube and all audio on SoundCloud; the site itself is text, a few images and the PDFs. A static site reproduces it fully.
- Every existing URL above must keep resolving after the move (bookmarks and shared links).
- The Culadasa teaching material is under Creative Commons BY 4.0 per the About page; carry the licence notice over.
- Darin's own prior WordPress-to-static migration is the model; GitHub Pages was the proposed free host.
- This repository (HumEx1/culadasa-website) is a temporary home: it will be transferred to the "Culadasa" GitHub organisation once that exists. Transfer keeps history and redirects.
- From Claude's cloud container, culadasa.com is not reachable (network allowlist); fetching the PDFs and images needs either an allowlist change or a download from D's machine.
