# Tasks for students and teachers

Work that makes the archive sound: checking, correcting and filling in. Each task can be taken by one person or split up. Hours are rough estimates for one careful person; most tasks split cleanly by video, session, document or page.

To take a task, tell the maintenance group which one and which part (for example "transcripts, videos 1–10"), so two people don't do the same work.

## 1. Video transcripts (99 videos, about 113 hours of video) — about 282 hours

The transcripts are YouTube's automatic captions with Pāli terms corrected by rule (`docs/TRANSCRIPTS.md`).

| Task | Hours |
|---|---|
| Correct the "John"s that mean jhāna. About 20 are left, because a fixed rule can't tell them from people's names (list with a search for "John" in `docs/transcripts/`) | 1 |
| Read each transcript for misheard Pāli, Sanskrit and Tibetan terms the rules missed (for example "Dupin ñāṇas" for dukkha ñāṇas). About 15 minutes per video | 25 |
| Proofread a transcript against its video: fix misheard words, names and numbers. About 2 hours per hour of video | 225 |
| Mark who is speaking in the 61 AMA sessions (questioner or Culadasa). About 30 minutes per session | 30 |
| Give the 31 videos that aren't on the Videos page their full titles (the download cut some titles short) | 1 |
