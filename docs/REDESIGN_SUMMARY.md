# The new Culadasa website: a summary

Preview: https://humex1.github.io/culadasa-website/redesign-2/
The current site, unchanged, for comparison: https://humex1.github.io/culadasa-website/

## 1. In short

The redesign turns culadasa.com from a set of pages into a place to **learn from**. It keeps everything Culadasa left (handouts, essays, talks, recordings, the Q&A archive, the teachers list) and adds ways to find your way through it:

- **A path for beginners** (Start here) and **a path for going deeper** (Learn).
- **Search across all of his teachings** at once, ranked by how often he talks about what you searched for.
- **A word cloud** of his most-used terms, so you can explore by clicking.
- **A teachers map** with "find a teacher near you".
- **A proper page for The Mind Illuminated**, with reviews and where to buy it.
- **One calm, consistent look**: his portrait first, the book's colours and textures, easy to read on a phone.

It costs nothing to host. Every old page address still works.

## 2. Home page and menus

**Top of every page**
- The **CULADASA** name, with "Upasaka Culadasa · John Yates, Ph.D. · 1945–2021" beneath it, on a faint pattern taken from the book cover.
- The menu sits underneath it: **Start here · Learn · Topics · Library · The Book · Teachers · Conversations · About**, plus a **Newsletter** button.
- **Library** opens a short list: Writings, Audio, Videos, Q&A archive.
- On a phone the menu folds into one **Menu** button.

**Each section has its own colour**, so you always know where you are. The colours run left to right like a paint-sample card: blue, grey-green, olive, mustard, sand (The Book), apricot, red, sage.

**The home page, top to bottom**
1. His portrait beside his family's words: *"That his work be freely and equally available to all students."*
2. Two buttons: **Start here** and **Search the teachings**.
3. **Find your way in**: four doors (Start here, Learn the method, Topics, Find a teacher).
4. **From the archive**: three of his talks, ready to play.
5. **The Mind Illuminated**: the book in brief.
6. **Culadasa in conversation**: interviews with him.
7. **Newsletter** sign-up, then the footer with his YouTube, SoundCloud, Facebook and Patreon links.

## 3. The new features, and how they work

**Start here**
- Seven simple steps for someone new: read the beginner's handout, prepare for each sit, try a guided meditation, learn the map, understand attention and awareness, take it into daily life, ask your question.
- Each step links straight to his own material for it.

**Learn**
- The teachings arranged as a journey: **the basics → the method → the science of mind → going deeper → daily life**.
- Under each heading are the handouts, essays and recordings that cover it, such as dullness, the ten stages, jhāna and the mind-system model.

**Topics: search everything he said**
- Type a word, such as "jhana", "dullness" or "compassion", and it searches all of his handouts, essays, the About page, the video titles and 439 recorded questions from the Q&A sessions: about 172,000 words.
- Results are ranked **only by how many times each source mentions the word**. There are no other rules and no editorial choices.
- Each result shows the matching passages with your word highlighted, and links to the source. For Q&A answers, the link opens the video at the exact moment the question is asked.
- Spelling is forgiving: "jhana" also finds "jhāna", and "compassion" also finds "compassionate".
- Buttons narrow the results by type (Handouts, Essays, Q&A and so on).

**The word cloud**
- His most-used meaningful words, sized by how often he uses them. Common words like "the" are left out.
- Click any word to search for it.

**Teachers: map and "near me"**
- A map shows where certified teachers are. Tap **Near me** and it shows the closest first (your browser asks permission and nothing is stored).
- Filter by **Teaches online** or **In person**, or search by name or place.
- Each teacher has their own page for their bio, photo, location and website. These are waiting for the teachers' details; the "Sample Teacher" pages are examples only.

**The Book**
- The Mind Illuminated in one place: what it is, the ten stages from the first sit to equanimity, who wrote it, what teachers and authors say about it, and a button to buy it from the publisher.

**Videos and audio**
- All 68 videos are shown as pictures with titles. A video loads only when you press play, so the page stays fast and nothing is sent to YouTube until then.
- The SoundCloud recordings are grouped: guided meditations, Q&As, teaching retreats, intensive retreats and Patreon Q&As.

**Q&A archive**
- All 61 online Q&A sessions, each listing its questions. Every question links to the moment in the video where it is asked. The index is credited to Alex.

**Conversations**
- Interviews and podcasts with Culadasa from around the web, each one checked to be real.

## 4. What else

**What stays the same**
- Every page address of the old site still works, including the PDFs, so links people saved or shared don't break.
- All of the old site's text and media are carried over.

**Built in**
- Works well on phones.
- Easy to use with a keyboard or a screen reader, and readable colour contrast throughout.
- Links that leave the site open in a new tab.
- A free licence notice (Creative Commons), as he wished.

**Still needed before it could go live**
- The teachers' own details for their pages: bio, photo, town, website, and their consent.
- Permissions to confirm: Alex for the Q&A index, the publisher or family for the cover art.
- A mailing service for the newsletter. The sign-up box is a placeholder and sends nothing yet.
- The group's choice of design, then the launch steps in `docs/SEO_AUDIT.md`.

**The plan**
The first launch is an exact copy of today's site, to show the move was done faithfully. The redesign follows only if, and when, the group chooses it.
