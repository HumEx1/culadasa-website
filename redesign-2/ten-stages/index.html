The book

The ten stages

Each stage of The Mind Illuminated, in Culadasa’s own words from his talks, AMA sessions, writings and recordings.

Open a stage for a video to start with and every place he talks about it. Many of these talks came after the book, so they add to it: questions students asked as they practised, and his answers. The book’s own overview of the ten stages is on pages 2–19 of the 2015 first edition.

  1. NoviceEstablishing a practice52 mentions
  2. NoviceInterrupted attention and overcoming mind-wandering94 mentions
  3. NoviceExtended attention and overcoming forgetting112 mentions
  4. SkilledContinuous attention and overcoming gross distraction and strong dullness262 mentions
  5. SkilledOvercoming subtle dullness and increasing mindfulness201 mentions
  6. SkilledSubduing subtle distraction180 mentions
  7. TransitionExclusive attention and unifying the mind151 mentions
  8. AdeptMental pliancy and pacifying the senses118 mentions
  9. AdeptMental and physical pliancy and calming the intensity of meditative joy61 mentions
  10. AdeptTranquility and equanimity58 mentions

Milestones: continuous attention to the meditation object (after stage 3); sustained exclusive focus of attention (after stage 6); effortless stability of attention (after stage 7); persistence of the mental qualities of an adept (after stage 10).

The videos to start with are chosen for now by where he talks about each stage longest. The teachers will choose them.
