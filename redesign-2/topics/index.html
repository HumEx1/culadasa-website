Search over a million words: every word of about 100 videos, 26 retreat handouts and essays, 441 AMA questions, and the titles and descriptions of 400+ audio recordings. Results are ranked by how often they mention what you search for.

Sources: his handouts and essays, the About page, the Culadasa Q&A Index (the questions from his AMA videos), the audio recordings’ titles and descriptions, and the transcripts of 99 videos. The transcripts are YouTube’s automatic captions with the Pāli terms corrected, so expect some mistakes; each result opens the video at the moment he says it. You can also type a whole question. Spellings with and without accents match each other, and so do the Pāli and Sanskrit forms of a word (jhāna and dhyāna, mettā and maitrī). Common English words are left out of the word cloud.
