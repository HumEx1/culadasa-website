Awakening & Insight - Buddhist Wisdom meets Brain Science - Culadasa

Transcript

YouTube’s automatic captions, with the Pāli terms it mishears corrected, so expect mistakes. Each time plays the video from that moment.

0:08 awakening is a cognitive event it's the attainment of wisdom it's the overcoming of ignorance this cognitive event comes about as a result of insight and insight is a transformation in the way that you understand something an inside experience forces your mind to revise the way that you understand reality it's a new piece of information that doesn't fit in the paradigm that you've been operating from awakening requires a series of insights and awakening itself involves a culminating insight in this series the first is insight into impermanence this is not this this doesn't mean impermanence in the sense that uh everything eventually disappears we already know that this is a radical insight into impermanence that indeed there are no things there is only process

1:12 the second insight is insight into emptiness this can be a difficult concept to grasp but basically what it is it's insight into the fact that that our mind determines the way we perceive ourselves and and the world emptiness is the insight that realizes that all we ever know are the stories that our mind weaves as a result of our experiences the third insight is insight into into suffering which is basically the that the insight that in a world where there are no things only process to cling to anything can only lead to suffering the next important insight is insight into the interconnectedness of everything everything without exception arises due to causes and conditions and that absolutely everything is in turn itself causes and conditions for other things

2:20 the degree of this interconnectedness is such that we could say that absolutely everything is the cause of everything the final insight the culminating insight is insight into no self that the self you think you are is just another empty mental fabrication along with all of the others and this is this is the culminating insight that produces awakening you
